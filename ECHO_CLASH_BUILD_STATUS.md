## Implemented, partially tested

- Spectator camera after elimination in Corebreak (code path runs in headless tests; not visually checked).
- Scoreboard (Tab) and pause panel: built and rendered in windowed runs, not exercised by a human yet.
- Relic Rush sudden death and the 5-minute ending: rule-level unit tests only.
- Bot use of Echo and dash: observed casts in logs; tactical quality not evaluated by a human.

## Not implemented

- Packaged `.exe`: export preset exists; templates were being downloaded at the time of writing (see TEST_PLAN for the packaging command).
- Internet matchmaking, accounts, relays (explicitly out of scope).
- Bots do not deliberately plan routes through phase gates or ziplines (they use ramps and, when stepped on, launch pads).
- Client-side movement prediction (clients feel one physics tick plus interpolation behind the server; acceptable on LAN).
- Minimap, settings for resolution (fullscreen toggle exists).

## Known issues

- Godot's class cache: after adding new `class_name` scripts, run the editor once or `tools\godot.ps1 --headless --import`, otherwise headless runs fail with "Identifier not declared".
- First host on a machine triggers the Windows Firewall prompt; declining it blocks LAN joins.
- Hard bots at close range can be lethal to an idle player; difficulty tuning is a playtest item.
