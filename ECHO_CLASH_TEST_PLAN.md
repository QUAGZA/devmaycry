## Known limitations found during testing

- Client movement is server-authoritative with ~1 physics tick plus interpolation delay (fine on LAN; noticeable on Wi-Fi hotspots with high latency).
- Bots do not deliberately route through phase gates or ziplines; they navigate the navmesh (ramps, decks) and use launch pads when they step on them.
- Sudden death in Relic Rush and the Relic results screen were verified by unit tests and a 100 s bot run, not by a full 5-minute match.
